Two fonts are bundled with Sous Chef so their look is the same on every computer:

UnifrakturMaguntia   the blackletter of the Pilgrimage titles       OFL_unifrakturmaguntia.txt
Bangers              the Comic strip's captions and sound effects   OFL_bangers.txt

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows them to be bundled and
redistributed with software. They have the Latin alphabet only; for other alphabets choose
other fonts in the Export window (Look › Title font and Text font).

Everything else uses fonts already on the computer.
